# Commercial licensing and services

Mechy combines an AGPL-3.0 open-source core with separately negotiated commercial
licensing. Its intended primary revenue sources are annual maintenance,
deployment and engineering verification services.

## Commercial licensing

A customer may request an alternative licence for proprietary integration,
distribution or operation where the customer does not wish to rely on the
AGPL grant. Any exception applies only to the code and rights expressly covered
by a signed agreement with the relevant rights holder or authorized licensor.
Third-party obligations remain in force.

This page is an invitation to discuss terms, not a licence grant or a binding
offer. An agreement must identify the parties, covered components and versions,
permitted uses and distribution, term, fees, update rights, and any obligations
for modifications. Neither a payment for services nor access to this repository
automatically grants alternative software rights.

Users who comply with AGPL do not need to buy a commercial licence. A commercial
licence cannot guarantee income from every enterprise adopter.

## Services

| Service | Scope to agree with the customer |
| --- | --- |
| Annual maintenance | Supported versions, maintenance period, fixes, dependency updates, regression reports and response arrangements |
| Deployment | Installation, private infrastructure, reproducible builds, compute and CAD/CAE integrations, and operational handover |
| Engineering verification | Model assumptions, reference cases, numerical checks, uncertainty and validity limits, and agreed evidence reports |
| Sponsored development | Agreed features, milestones, acceptance criteria, documentation and upstream contribution rights |

Prices, delivery dates, response times, support capacity and acceptance criteria
are specified in the customer agreement. These descriptions do not assert that
the current prototype already provides validated propulsion calculations or
certified engineering outcomes. Verification work must distinguish analytical
predictions, simulation results and measured evidence.

## Keeping improvements open

Our development policy is to maintain the public core and bring general-purpose
fixes and improvements back to its main development line. Sponsored work should
agree up front which general-purpose changes may be published under AGPL and
commercially licensed, and which customer-specific code, data and parameters
remain confidential. Customer material is not published without the necessary
permission.

Commercial customers' modification and contribution duties come from their
signed agreements; they are not automatically imposed by the AGPL alternative.
Community contributions follow [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).
